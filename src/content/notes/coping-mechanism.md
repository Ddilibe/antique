---
title: 'Coping Mechanism'
date: 2026-11-15
tags: ['emotions', 'self']
---

I don't know what stirred up my emotions, but today, I felt something. Maybe it was a girl, or perhaps my mother. Whatever it was, something stirred within me, and for once, I felt it.

I am sympathetic, perhaps even empathetic, towards other people's situations, but I don't know if I genuinely care or if I have simply learned to play the part. I have buried my emotions so deep that I sometimes convince myself they no longer exist. Yet, every now and then, certain situations bring them erupting to the surface like a volcano.

The question is, why? Should I allow myself to express these emotions in the moment, or should I exercise self-restraint and bury them once again?

Perhaps the best response to this question lies in a quote by **Sanjo Jendayi**:

> Don't allow triggers  
> To rob you of sound judgement.  
> Question your feelings.
>
> ― **Sanjo Jendayi**
