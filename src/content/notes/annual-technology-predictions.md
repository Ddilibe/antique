One of my favorite YouTubers, Lauriewired, does something I find particularly interesting: making annual predictions about the technology ecosystem.

It is something I would like to start doing myself. With the year coming to an end, this is a note to self: **make annual predictions about the direction of the tech industry.**

I want to predict how technology will evolve, which technologies will gain prominence, who the major players will be, and which companies will dominate different areas of the industry.

These predictions won't just be random guesses. I want to document my reasoning and revisit each prediction at the end of every quarter to evaluate how accurate I was and what I might have overlooked.

The goal is to build a habit of observing the tech ecosystem, anticipating trends, and developing a better understanding of where the industry is heading.
